## About The Cosmere

The Cosmere is a shared universe spanning multiple fantasy series by Brandon Sanderson. Each world has its own magic system, but all are connected by a deeper underlying framework. Characters known as "Worldhoppers" travel between worlds, and keen readers can spot them appearing across different series.

**Worlds include**: Scadrial (Mistborn), Roshar (Stormlight Archive), Nalthis (Warbreaker), Sel (Elantris), Taldain (White Sand), and Lumar (Tress).

## Getting Started

New to the Cosmere? Start with the **Mistborn Era 1** trilogy for a complete, self-contained story. Then dive into **The Stormlight Archive** for Sanderson's epic magnum opus.

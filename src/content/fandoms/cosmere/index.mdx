## About The Cosmere

Think the Marvel Cinematic Universe, but for Brandon Sanderson's fantasy books. Many of the author's novels take place in a shared universe and tell stories of the people and civilizations of that universe's planets. The Cosmere includes worlds like Scadrial (Mistborn), Roshar (Stormlight Archive), Nalthis (Warbreaker), and Sel (Elantris).

Many Cosmere fans expect these worlds to discover one another (or be united by a mysterious force) in an Avengers-style crossover series. Characters known as "Worldhoppers" already travel between these worlds, and keen readers can spot them appearing across different series.

## Magic Systems

Each world in the Cosmere has its own unique magic system, but all are connected by a deeper underlying framework:

- **Allomancy & Feruchemy** (Scadrial) - Metal-based powers
- **Surgebinding** (Roshar) - Bonding with spren for supernatural abilities
- **Awakening** (Nalthis) - Using Breath to animate objects
- **AonDor** (Sel) - Symbol-based magic

## Getting Started

Start with the **Mistborn Era 1** trilogy for a complete, satisfying story that introduces Cosmere concepts. Then dive into **The Stormlight Archive** for Sanderson's epic magnum opus.

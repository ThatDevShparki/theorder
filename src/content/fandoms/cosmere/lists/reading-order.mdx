## Why This Order?

This reading order is designed to:

1. **Start accessible** — Mistborn Era 1 is a complete, self-contained trilogy
2. **Build gradually** — Introduce Stormlight after you're hooked on Sanderson's style
3. **Maximize payoffs** — Place Warbreaker before Words of Radiance for key crossover moments
4. **Respect reveals** — Arcanum Unbounded contains spoilers for earlier books and setup for later ones
5. **End epic** — Save far-future stories for when you have full Cosmere context

## Wild Cards: Elantris & White Sand

Both can be moved around based on preference:

- **Elantris**: Sanderson's first novel. Some prefer it first to see his growth; others find later works more polished. Just read it before Rhythm of War.
- **White Sand**: A graphic novel that works whenever you want a quick win between larger volumes.

## Notes

- **Edgedancer** (included in Arcanum Unbounded) bridges Words of Radiance and Oathbringer
- **Mistborn: Secret History** (also in Arcanum Unbounded) has Era 1 spoilers and is best read after The Hero of Ages
- If planning to read **Isles of the Emberdark**, you can skip the novella Sixth of the Dusk in Arcanum Unbounded (the story is included in Isles with slight tweaks)

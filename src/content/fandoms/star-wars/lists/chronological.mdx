## How to Use This List

This comprehensive chronological list presents the complete Star Wars saga in timeline order—from the ancient origins of the Jedi through the events after the Skywalker Saga.

| Type                       | Description                   |

| _Temporary Titles_         | Upcoming/unannounced projects |

## Notes

- **The Clone Wars placement**: The final four episodes of The Clone Wars Season 7 (The Siege of Mandalore arc) happen simultaneously with Revenge of the Sith.
- **Clone Wars 2003**: The 2003 Genndy Tartakovsky series is no longer canon, but a fan edit preserves elements that bridge well with current canon.
- **Andor & Rebels**: While these series overlap chronologically, it's recommended to watch Rebels first, then Andor directly before Rogue One for maximum narrative impact.
- **The Book of Boba Fett**: Must be watched between The Mandalorian Season 2 and Season 3 as it contains essential story crossover.
- **Resistance**: Season 1 episodes 1-19 happen before The Force Awakens, while episodes 20-21 happen during/after it.

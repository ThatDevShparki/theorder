## How to Use This List

This list presents the Star Wars saga in the order events occur in the timeline. Starting with The Phantom Menace, you'll follow Anakin Skywalker's journey from a young slave on Tatooine to his transformation into Darth Vader, and finally his redemption through his son Luke.

## Notes

- **First time?** Some fans prefer the Release Order for a classic viewing experience.
- **Machete Order**: An alternative that skips Episode I and watches II, III after V for a dramatic reveal.

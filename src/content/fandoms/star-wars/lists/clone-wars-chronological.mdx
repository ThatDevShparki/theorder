## How to Use This List

Star Wars: The Clone Wars aired out of chronological order. This list presents all 133 episodes plus the theatrical movie in the order events actually occur in the timeline, making the story arcs and character development much easier to follow.

This chronological order is based on the official StarWars.com viewing guide and places the events between Episode II: Attack of the Clones and Episode III: Revenge of the Sith.

## Notes

- **Theatrical Movie**: The Clone Wars movie serves as an early introduction to Ahsoka Tano and should be watched after "Hidden Enemy"
- **The Siege of Mandalore**: The final four episodes run parallel to Revenge of the Sith and contain some of the most emotionally powerful Star Wars content ever created
- **First time?** This chronological order is recommended for first-time viewers as it presents a more coherent narrative

## How to Use This List

This is the classic way to experience Star Wars. Start with the original 1977 film and follow Luke Skywalker's journey. Then go back in time with the prequels to see Anakin Skywalker's transformation into Darth Vader.

## Notes

- **Recommended for first-timers**: This order preserves all the major plot twists as they were originally intended.
- The Empire Strikes Back reveal hits differently when you don't know the backstory.
